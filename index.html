About the Archive

Sixty-nine iterations
of a single idea.

Wing Archive is a closed body of work — exactly 69 pieces, each a 1/1 original, each built from the same foundational motif. The archive explores how a single idea transforms under different forces: weight, time, material, and memory.

Each relic is a document of change. Nothing is repeated. Everything is related.

01
Change
Every form departs from the last. No two states are the same.
02
Growth
The motif expands, contracts, and finds new surface.
03
Remains
What survives each transformation becomes the archive.
04
Relic
Each piece is evidence — of a moment, a process, an end.
